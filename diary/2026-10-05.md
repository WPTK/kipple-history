## The issue and housekeeping meeting

The owner went through every open issue. His rulings are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and
[MEETINGS.md](MEETINGS.md) (meeting 51). In short:

- Beta.2 stays perf-only. Beta.3 is the fixes: #241 (cap the folder label length before the path is resolved), #244 (OPML
