## Badges and the Scorecard

- The owner asked what could raise the OpenSSF Scorecard (7.1, then 7.3 after the first changes). I went through the
  checks and their weights. What a one-person project can fix: pinned actions, token permissions, the security policy,
  CodeQL, fuzzing, signed releases, the Best Practices badge. What it cannot: Code-Review and the higher
  Branch-Protection tiers, which need a second reviewer.
- The badge row came out of that. Go Report Card is retired, so no badge. The ghcr-badge `latest_tag` shows a signature
  tag and `size` errors on a multi-arch image, so the tags badge is used. For a views counter I tested four services and
  chose hits.sh. #160 (merged 13:40) refreshed the row and removed the SemVer badge; he wanted it back, and #161 (merged
  14:49) kept it. #161 also added `scorecard.yml` and the Codecov upload, with the token as a secret he added and
  `codecov.yml` set to informational. Nothing CI gates on changed.
- He asked whether SemVer 2.0.0-rc.2 differs from 2.0.0. For Kipple, no. The differences are build metadata handling,
  leading zeros, empty identifiers and the BNF. We stay on 2.0.0.

## The iOS blur

- A soft blur above the list header icons until you scroll, on iOS 27 in Safari and in the installed app. I found no
  blur in the app's CSS, the header is opaque, and `theme-color` already equals the background in all 20 schemes. The
  diagnosis: iOS tints and softens the status-bar strip from whatever is at the top edge of the page.
- Fix #163: a solid fixed element, `#kp-top-cover`, as tall as the notch inset. To test it on his iPhone I ran a
  throwaway preview instance bound to the Tailscale address on port 7083, using a new `KIPPLE_DEV_HOST` option in
  `web/scripts/seed.mjs`. He confirmed: "That fixes it". Merged 20:07 on 09-30.
