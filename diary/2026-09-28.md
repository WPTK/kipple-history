## Beta.1 feedback triage

the owner attached his own testing notes from his phone (`0.3.0B1 feedback.md`, ten items) and asked for
triage, a plan, and anything the feedback pointed at beyond its own list. Sorted into: two quick fixes
(the "N new articles" pill firing on background polls, not just a manual refresh; a mobile tab-bar seam
against image-forward content), two feature gaps that direct code inspection confirmed rather than just
took his word for (folders cannot collapse on the only feed-browsing surface mobile actually has - the
persistent sidebar with its collapse chevron simply does not render below the desktop width breakpoint;
Manage Feeds shows a drag handle and an edit pencil on every row with no way to hide either), two small new
features (a "Manage this feed" entry in an article's menu, opening the same feed editor Feed Health already
uses; a Select mode for Feed Health's bulk actions, mirroring one Manage Feeds already had), and two that
needed a design pass rather than a code fix (settings reorganization; list-layout differentiation, where a
side-by-side browser-pane comparison confirmed his complaint - Editorial and Cards genuinely converge to
near-identical at narrow widths, since Cards has no card chrome of its own). Filed as issues #55-#62, all
tagged and milestoned. One item ("all Suite 3 testing passes from my phone") closed out risk-register R1
and issue #30 directly rather than needing a fix.

Four PRs shipped the same stretch (#63-#66), each checked against the live seeded app in the browser pane
as well as the test suite - not just trusting green CI. A live bug turned up outside the feedback list
while doing that: tapping a feed or folder row in Manage Feeds seemed not to navigate, in the browser pane,
at both mobile and desktop widths. Chased it far enough to conclude the pane's own click-coordinate mapping
was unreliable that session (a plain JS `.click()` on the same element worked; an unrelated click earlier
the same session had mis-hit by over 100px) rather than a real product bug, and did not file it without a
real-device confirmation.
