# Diary, 2026-09-27 (session started the evening of 2026-09-26)

## Phase 4 pre-meeting, then alpha.4

- Pre-meeting held first, one topic at a time with a recommendation each (MEETINGS.md 26, DECISIONS.md "Phase 4 stats
  decisions"). the owner steered three things: habits at the top of the page with sources and pruning as the focus; a simple
  yearly Wrapped with an opt-in share sheet and a stats off switch; the web app is the intended client, Reeder stats
  tracking goes to the parking lot. He asked for outside research on measuring engaged time without gamifying it: the
  analytics literature (Chartbeat, GA4, MDN and Chrome page-lifecycle guidance) matched our design; the one addition was
  a 2 minute idle cutoff.
- Everything he was asked to decide he agreed to; he asked for "everything in the first cut" for export and added four
  easy wins (summary strip, daily chart, average read length, quick-bounce rate). Comparison and read rate per feed were
  dropped as too much lift for the return.
- Build (branch phase4-stats-sender, PR #17): two Sonnet agents in parallel (server, web). The web work then exposed
  a real bug: a second observer on the bootstrap query with `skipToken` replaced the query's fetch function, breaking
  later refetches. Existing tests caught it, not a new one.
- High-effort review (eight Opus finders plus a verifier) found ten defects worth fixing, among them replayed
  share/open_original events being counted twice (two tabs, or a lost response) and scroll depth being read against the
  wrong article. The fix changed one agreed detail: the per-session sequence number became a random id on every event
  with a unique index (migration 0008 was undeployed, so it was edited in place). A second review of the merged diff
  found eleven smaller items; all fixed. A row-collapse test that failed on `main` about two runs in three was a
  test-only timing race and was made deterministic.
- What I learned: reviewers converge on the same defects from different angles, which is stronger evidence than a single
  verifier; a fix that changes a contract detail the owner agreed to should be said out loud rather than absorbed.
- Open: CI result on PR #17; a device check that `document.hasFocus()` is true in an installed iPhone web app (if it is
  not, reading time on his phone would record as zero); `review3search.test.tsx` timed out once under heavy load;
  alpha.5 (the stats screen) not started; no deploy without his go-ahead.
