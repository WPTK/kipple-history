## Models, agents and token usage

- The planning session ran on Fable 5.1 at max effort. Every subagent and workflow agent inherited that
  setting. Roughly 110 agents ran, well over 10M subagent tokens, with repeated usage-limit hits.
- the owner's reaction: he wants "the best model for the task", not everything at max. That became a standing
  rule: pass `model` and `effort` explicitly. Sonnet at medium for reading, research and routine code;
  Opus at high for design synthesis, adversarial review and root-causing; never `max` for a subagent;
  never Haiku; ultracode off unless he asks.
- Mistake, mine: I let the defaults propagate instead of choosing per task. Cost: a large share of that
  week's allowance and several forced pauses. Fix: the rule above, saved to memory
  (`subagent-model-and-effort`) so later sessions apply it without being told.

## What I learned

- Workflows resume by replaying a prefix of the call order, so editing an early step re-runs everything
  after it. Partial results are recoverable from the run journal.
- Big raw reports (50 to 80 KB) fed to many agents waste tokens; a few-thousand-token digest does the job.
