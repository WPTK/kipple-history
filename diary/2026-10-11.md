# 2026-10-11 (Sunday): 0.9.0-beta.1

17 commits to main, the release commit #392 (09:36, tag `v0.9.0-beta.1` on `ba007c3`) and 13 changelog fragments folded in. No schema
change; schema stays 19. The release commit is docs-tier, so CI on that head was the gate; the code gates ran on `8a24004`
(Go twice, fuzz, web, changelog, starter feeds, Browser UAT Suite 1 on #391).

## What changed

- **Keyboard.** The article list is one Tab stop (#369, #379), so Tab gets past it. Before, every article was its own Tab stop and
  the list loads more as focus moves down, so in a big library Tab never got past the list. Review ran three rounds and left four
  low findings (#390). When the focused article leaves the list, focus moves to the article that takes its place; #391 limited that rescue to focus that was inside the leaving row.
- **Mute similar (#374, #381).** Saving the filter also mutes the article it was started from when the filter matches it. A
  starred article stays starred. When the article was not muted, the confirmation says why in one sentence. Restore in Muted puts
  back the read state the article had.
- **Undo never un-mutes (#391).** The server decides. See CHALLENGES 88.
- **Images (#375, #380; #382, #383).** An image Kipple will not display is replaced by an "Image not shown" note with a link to the
  original. A picture that leads three or more items of one fetch is treated as the site's (a logo, a banner), and those items
  take their next picture.
- **Setup restore (#372, #378).** A backup uploaded in one browser but never confirmed no longer locks a second browser out of the
  wizard for up to an hour.
- **Wording (#373, #377, #402; #394, #396, #401).** One phrase for a folder left with no feeds, and a Muted intro and delete dialog
  that say what is true.
- **Saved searches (#386, #389).** If the list cannot be loaded the sidebar says so, with Try again, instead of dropping the
  section.
- **Stats (#393, #405).** Leaving an article by reload or typed address records the reading time. See CHALLENGES 86.
- **Release tooling.** `release-publish` verifies signatures with an empty Docker config (#399); dependency groups bumped (#324,
  #349, #365); one `useLatestRef` hook replaces refs refreshed in passive effects (#387, #400).
