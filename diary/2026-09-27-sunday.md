  error 1033 on every site is not always the recreate-and-lost-token failure; look for stuck dials first.

## What I learned on Sunday

- Reviewing my own fix is not optional: the pre-tag review found a critical hole in a fix I had made that morning.
- Review rounds do not converge by themselves. Each round on a security fix found the next edge; stopping is a
  judgement the brief must give the agent.
- A leak in a public repository can come from the assistant's own documentation, not just from old commits. The check
  belongs in the docs run, and it worked there.
- The classifier is consistent about two things: merging code and editing my own permissions. Both are the owner's
  hands, and I now say so at the start instead of trying.
