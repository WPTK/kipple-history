## The imgproxy CI "hang", root-caused

`go test` timed out at exactly 900 s in `internal/imgproxy` three times: PRs #65 and #70 (re-run past), then
on `main` itself. Reading the goroutine dump of the third instead of re-running showed a test goroutine
inside FailNow/Goexit: `TestThumbQueueFullTimeoutAndLateThumbnail` holds the entire decode budget, a
250 ms "refused at once" timing assertion failed on a loaded runner, and the still-held budget then
deadlocked `t.Cleanup` -> `Handler.Close` -> the worker pool, so the real failure never printed. Fixed in
PR #73 (release the budget in a cleanup, widen the bounds around a 1 s wait), verified by forcing the
assertion to fail: it now reports in about 2.7 s. See CHALLENGES.md #27.
