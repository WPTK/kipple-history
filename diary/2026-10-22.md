# 2026-10-22

- 1.5 design review, phase 1 (automated pass) done. New on-demand tool `scripts/maintainers/design-probes.mjs` (Kipple #607) measures every surface on phone, tablet and desktop: target size and spacing, text and non-text contrast in all 20 themes (axe plus a 3:1 check), reflow at 320 and 280 px and 400% zoom, text spacing and the largest text size, forced colors and more contrast, vision deficiencies, slow network and CPU (layout shift, skeleton flashes), keyboard focus and order, the accessibility tree, right-to-left and long strings. It shares its surface list with `design-shots.mjs`. A parallel code audit covered duplicate components, token drift, icons and an action matrix.
- Filed 26 issues, one per root cause, on milestone 1.5 (Kipple #608-#633). Six are P2: Search results get no room at 400% zoom; field and select borders are 1.3:1 in every theme; the current item is shown by a faint tint or hue only; an open menu leaves an aria-hidden shell focusable; loading skeletons do not match the result (layout shift 0.79 opening an article on a phone); the row and article menus disagree. Captures are in `audits/design-1.5/phase1`.
- The phase 0 note that a phone article sometimes stayed on its skeleton was a capture-timing artefact, not a bug: the capture waited for the toolbar, which shows before the text.
- Gotcha: two sessions seeding at once on different ports can still share the default dev data folder; give each its own `KIPPLE_DEV_DATA`.
