**Fuzz and test assets to build (Phase 1 deliverables)**
0. **Owner-data rule for all assets:** the owner's five OPMLs, any real-subscription-derived corpus and anything

1. **Large OPML set** (generated by a script under `scripts/` with a Pester test, reproducible from a seed, output

   - `huge-flat.opml`: 10,000 feeds, no folders.
   - `huge-nested.opml`: 5,000 feeds in 500 folders, nested 1 to 12 levels deep, plus a pathological 200-level file.
   - `wide-folder.opml`: one folder with 3,000 feeds.
   - A realistic one: merge and de-duplicate the owner's five OPMLs plus public feeds (`kipple-all.opml` 134,
     `feedly-2024-02-15` 111) up to a few thousand real, live-checked feeds.
   - Hostile variants: duplicate and near-duplicate URLs (case, trailing slash, `www.`, http vs https, tracking
     params, fragments), same title different URL, empty titles, 10 MB titles, emoji/RTL/combining/zero-width
     characters, invalid XML entities, CDATA, unknown attributes, `xmlUrl` missing or relative, `file://`, `javascript:`,
     private-IP and localhost URLs, URLs with credentials, over-long URLs, huge category attributes, BOM and UTF-16/
     Latin-1 declared encodings, XXE and billion-laughs payloads, mixed OPML 1.0/2.0, `<outline>` with children and a
     feed URL at once, truncated files, files with NULs.
   - Use them for: import time and memory, the folder and dedupe logic (`feedurl.Key`), the import UI with progress,
     export and re-import round trip equals original (property test), and as `FuzzParse`/`FuzzImportExport` seeds.
2. **Feed corpus** (as above) plus a generated set of synthetic feeds: 10,000-entry feed, 50 MB feed, entries with
   huge content, thousands of enclosures, deeply nested HTML, duplicate GUIDs, GUID reuse across feeds, future and

   deflate and brotli bodies, chunked encodings, slow-drip servers, redirect chains and loops, 304 variations,
   wrong Content-Type, HTML served as a feed, feed served inside HTML (discovery).
3. **HTML/sanitizer corpus:** real article HTML from the corpus, the public XSS cheat-sheet style payload list
